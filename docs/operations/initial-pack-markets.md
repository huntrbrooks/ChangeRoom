**Pack opening revision, 5 November 2026.** The app enables one-time packs only on the canonical hosted production configuration or an explicitly isolated sandbox. Provider firewall readback and storefront verification determine when public sales actually open. The ordinary production try-on passed: one nonprivileged test credit paid for one completed look in 14.06 seconds; downloading and reopening did not spend another credit. The three one-time packs, decline/retry and refund/replay passed in the earlier Stripe sandbox rehearsal. The market-specific checks are recorded below.

There is no new subscription or standalone Payment Link. Other countries are outside this launch. No new payment service, Radar subscription, live test purchase or tax registration is authorized by this procedure.

The [Australian Business Register](https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View?id=11678658823) listed FRESH’N UP AU PTY LTD as active and not currently GST-registered on 5 November. Registration status does **not** establish whether the company should be registered. Do not infer company-wide past or projected activity from ChangeRoom’s Stripe account. Record only the owner’s threshold confirmation or range privately, not turnover figures in the repository.

## Before taking payment

1. Require the signed-in customer’s residence/personal-use declaration and the same country in the Vercel-managed request geolocation. Reject unsupported, conflicting or missing evidence before creating a Checkout Session. Never add a production test header, query parameter or default country. [Vercel header](https://vercel.com/docs/headers/request-headers#x-vercel-ip-country)
2. For eligible attempts, create only a known one-time, full-price AUD card Checkout, with billing address required, adaptive pricing off and manual capture. The signed webhook checks current Session, PaymentIntent, Charge, owner, amount, pack and billing country. Allow only the matching country; cancel an ineligible owned authorization, give no credits and do not collect funds. Customers may see a temporary bank hold. Store country codes and the policy version with settlement, not raw IP addresses or street addresses in the credit ledger. [Stripe authorization and capture](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method)
3. After full capture, grant credits exactly once. Replays and the authenticated return must not capture twice or grant on an authorization. The return page distinguishes incomplete/canceled purchases from confirmed payments. Preserve previously paid legitimate purchases and normal billing, cancellation, refund reconciliation and signed callbacks.

**Money collected or outcome uncertain, but no usable credits:** the signed callback returns a retryable error. Inspect the failed Stripe delivery and look up the zero-credit ledger adjustment with `metadata.reason = 'pack_payment_review'`; it records the reason, Session and PaymentIntent without changing the wallet. Check the current Charge, amount captured/refunded, original grant and any closure before taking action. Never tell the customer to pay again, silently grant to a deleted account or infer a refund from a retry. If funds were captured and delivery is impossible, Gerard owns the support case and appropriate remedy. Record the resolution privately, then verify the provider outcome, balance and replay. Use the [full-refund reconciliation procedure](customer-support.md#reconciling-a-completed-full-pack-refund) only if an original grant exists and it matches that procedure; disputed or partially refunded cases require individual review.

## Opening checks and ongoing responsibility

**5 November staging result (web `8f38f00`, protected sandbox):** A real AU request reached hosted Checkout. The A$14.99 Starter charge was captured once, the wallet went from 107 to 117, and the owner return and a redelivered Stripe event left the balance unchanged. Stripe’s ordinary hosted receipt showed one 10-credit pack, A$14.99 paid, the legal seller, ABN and “No GST charged.” This is test mode; no receipt-email delivery or company turnover is implied. Australian consumers must receive a receipt for a purchase over A$75, which includes the Pro pack; see the [ACCC receipt requirements](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/receipts-bills-proof-of-purchase). Verify that a signed-in buyer can open their own receipt from Billing after leaving Checkout and that a different buyer cannot. Do not rely on an administrator’s receipt link or promise a particular email was delivered.

The same deployed app canceled an AU order submitted with NZ billing: the original completion and Charge recorded NZ, the provider recorded the application’s cancellation, and return plus redelivery produced zero captured funds and no wallet change. Declaring NZ on an AU connection, including with a forged country header, was refused before a Stripe Session was created. The protected AU test exercises real Vercel geolocation; a controlled NZ settlement fixture cannot demonstrate native NZ egress.

The controlled NZ fixture subsequently captured the same A$14.99 once, granted 10 credits exactly once, and survived both owner return and signed event redelivery. On receipt revision `bc2373b`, the signed-in test buyer reopened the same AU receipt from Billing. An anonymous visitor and a different buyer could not open it, and the canceled order had no paid receipt. No live card was needed for those tests. Record the owner’s eligibility decision privately and revisit it at each review; no customer or accountant input is kept in this repository.
