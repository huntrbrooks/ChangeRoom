## Proportionate follow-up, not blanket launch blockers

- **Measured costs:** retain the preliminary matching-model usage observation and operate within the already approved US$7 worker, US$5/month cron planning allowance and US$25/month OpenAI limit, including up to US$5 for launch tests. Keep provider limits/alerts in force and stop additional testing or new purchases when the approved spending envelope or attended support coverage cannot be maintained. Reconcile attributable delivered-image costs, failed attempts, shared invoices and margins as reporting becomes available; US$0.02 is not a guaranteed unit price. This does not require a fresh commercial approval within existing limits. Seek approval only for changed commitments, limits or additional paid services.
- **Real iPhone/Safari HEIF:** remains unverified. Launch claims must accurately support JPG/PNG with conversion guidance. Prioritize real-device validation before broad mobile marketing; synthetic desktop HEIC success is not device certification.
- **Capacity:** one active generation is the chosen operating limit. Bounded memory and reject-before-claim tests pass. A two-request hosted busy/recovery smoke is useful if readily available; five simultaneous paid model calls are not a launch requirement. Measure real latency/failure rates before scaling.
- **Recovery:** confirm current settings/access and suppression/reconciliation instructions. An isolated restore is the appropriate evidence; overwriting live production for a drill is not required. Another production-copy drill can follow material storage changes.
- **Staffing:** one accessible accountable operator is sufficient for this scope without an SLA. A second trained operator is a resilience improvement; do not publish 24/7 coverage without it.
- **Receipts and subscriptions:** do not promise receipt-email delivery until tested. Hosted payment confirmation/Billing plus support are the minimum. With no live subscriptions and acquisition closed, a fresh paid renewal/cancellation purchase is unnecessary.
- **Security/legacy:** retain the [dependency review's](dependency-review-2026-11-04.md) unresolved native-library findings and reachability limits, with a maintenance owner/date. Keep legacy serving disabled and track unresolved inventory. A confirmed serious reachable issue or actual unauthenticated media exposure would block opening; raw scan counts or an incomplete historical inventory alone do not prove either.
- **Later scale work:** automatic refund-credit adjustment, multi-instance queues, replicas, multi-region recovery and a larger support organization are conditional improvements, not prerequisites for this limited pack launch.
