## Which application is being released?

The deployed subscriber flow issues an owner-bound job via Next.js `POST /api/try-on/hold`. The browser sends the job and photos to the separate Render FastAPI worker; after settlement, the signed-in customer can retrieve `GET /api/try-on/jobs/[id]/result` or list unexpired looks at `GET /api/try-on/recent`. PostgreSQL records funding, status and the private result. See [subscriber job funding, role grants and expiry](../../database/TRYON_JOBS.md). Check the release manifest before a rollout. The documented legacy Next `/api/try-on` and `/api/upload-urls` do not exist in the November baseline. Older builds used browser-managed credit holds and browser-driven finalization. Do not unblock purchases on those builds. Keep customer billing management and verified webhooks available during an incident.

2. If generation or entitlements cannot be trusted: refuse **new Checkout creation** at the edge and app, audit/disable only Stripe Payment Links that exclusively sell this product, re-list open app sessions. Existing subscriptions/credits, portal cancellation and signed webhooks must remain accessible. Do not bulk-cancel, refund, change plans or email users without owner approval. Keep a private inventory of previously active link IDs only, separately from credentials.
3. Compare provider incident history/limits and both release SHAs. Repoint frontend and Render to compatible known-good revisions **only behind the payment gate**. Do not roll back to unenforced generation, legacy public photo serving or unsigned checkout/credits. Normally keep additive database changes. If compatibility is uncertain, keep generation off, serve status/billing and fix forward. Do not down-migrate/destroy financial rows during incident response.
4. For a report of missing credits, cross-check ownership, Stripe paid/refunded status, billing ledger request/receipt and the job result. Retry the signed Stripe event only after fixing the cause; verify exactly one credit grant. Update card or cancel through `/billing`; confirm the portal’s effective date and no next charge. Do not promise a refund before owner review. For media deletion verify account ownership, inspect/reconcile the closure queue, object references and external providers; report exceptions accurately.
5. Rehearse the failing case, plus a cold start, failure recovery, sign-in, existing-account billing and cancellation in staging. Record operator sign-off before any reopening. Share only aggregate data with public status; keep redacted incident timeline, receipts and deploy IDs privately.
